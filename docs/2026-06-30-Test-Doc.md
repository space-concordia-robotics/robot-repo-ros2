## Conclusion of First Phase of Testing

Based on test results, the BAB appears to cut power under certain load/current conditions. However, behavior is inconsistent with earlier successful runs, so a firmware-related issue is also possible.
Update: Ben fixed the issue, Anthony was right something was limitting the current. It was the power supply on the wall was configured  200mA
Lesson: Always check the current on the PSU.
